The coffee grinder arrived on Thursday via UPS. The outer shipping carton was slightly crushed on one corner, but the molded foam inside kept the grinder safe. After using it for three mornings with medium roast beans, the 18 grind settings produce a very consistent texture for pour-over, though static makes fine grounds cling to the plastic chute. The motor is surprisingly quiet, but the hopper only holds about 40 grams. Overall solid build quality for $45.
